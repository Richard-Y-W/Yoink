# Yoink Drop Art Pack Expansion - 2026-07-07

This expansion adds 34 new collectible renders to the Yoink art pack.

- 4 Ultra Rare Holo Finds foil cards
- 10 Pocket Tech variants and new tech toys
- 10 Desk Pets variants and new mascots
- 10 Snack Relics variants and new prize toys

All entries were added to `item-catalog.json`, with prompt files in `prompts/` and PNG renders in `renders/`.

## Quality Pass

On 2026-07-07, the 30 non-Holo expansion renders were replaced with built-in image-generation outputs to match the polish level of the Ultra Rare Holo foil cards. The earlier local renderer outputs were backed up to `renders/backup-low-quality-2026-07-07/`, and the old renderer script was moved to `tools/archive/generate_expansion_2026_07_07_low_quality_renderer.py` so it cannot accidentally overwrite the generated assets.
